COMMUNITY RULES

Keep the community competitive, fair and welcoming.

01. Be Respectful

Treat everyone with kindness and respect. Bullying, harassment and hateful language are not allowed.

02. Keep It Family Friendly

Keep conversations appropriate for all ages. Avoid inappropriate, offensive or explicit content.

03. No Cheating

Hacks, cheats, unfair modifications and exploits are strictly prohibited.

04. No Spamming

Do not flood channels with repeated messages, emojis, stickers, GIFs or excessive caps.

05. Voice Chat

Keep voice chat respectful and appropriate. Avoid shouting or disruptive noises.

06. Use The Right Channels

Keep discussions in the appropriate channels so the server stays organized.

07. No Unauthorized Advertising

Do not advertise other Discord servers, websites or social media without staff approval.

08. Fair Play

Play fairly, be a good teammate and do not intentionally ruin another player's experience.

09. Respect Privacy

Never share another person's private information without their permission.

10. Listen To Staff

Follow reasonable staff instructions and handle disagreements through the proper channels.
