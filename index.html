Community Guidelines

Community Rules

Help keep LBBW fun, friendly, and welcoming for everyone.

01

Be Respectful

Treat everyone with kindness and respect. No bullying, harassment, or hateful language.

02

Keep It Family Friendly

Keep conversations appropriate for all ages. Avoid inappropriate, offensive, or explicit content.

03

No Cheating

Hacks, cheats, unfair modifications, exploits, and encouraging cheats are not allowed.

04

No Spamming

Do not flood chat with repeated messages, emojis, stickers, GIFs, excessive caps, or similar spam.

05

Voice Chat

Be respectful while speaking and avoid disruptive noises or inappropriate microphone use.

06

Use the Right Channels

Keep discussions in their appropriate channels.

07

Advertising

Do not advertise other Discord servers, websites, or social media without staff approval.

08

Fair Play

Play fairly and be a good teammate. Do not intentionally ruin games for others.

09

Profiles

Usernames, profile pictures, and statuses should be appropriate for everyone.

10

Respect Privacy

Never share another person's private information.

11

Listen to Staff

Follow staff instructions and discuss disagreements politely through the proper channels.

12

Reporting Issues

Report rule-breaking through the correct channels and provide honest information.

13

Consequences

Consequences can include reminders, warnings, timeouts, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans depending on the situation.

★

Community Values

Welcome new players, help others, show good sportsmanship, and have fun.

Important: LBBW is not an official Lifeboat server. This is an unofficial Lifeboat BedWars community.
Testing Guidelines

Tier Testing Rules

Rules and requirements for players requesting a tier test.

01

Request a Tier Test

Create a ticket in the tier-testing ticket channel and wait for a Tier Tester to arrange your test.

02

Testing Format

Tests are first to 5 points. If the player kills the tester and destroys their bed, the player earns a point. If the tester or another player kills the testee, the tester earns a point.

03

No Teaming

Teaming with other players is not allowed during testing.

04

No Item Restrictions

All items and tactics are permitted unless specifically stated otherwise.

05

Banned Maps

Solo tier testing does not allow Jungle, Frost Solos, or Enchanted Cavern.

06

Redos & Disconnections

Redos may be granted for qualifying lag-outs with video evidence or if a hacker is present in the match.

07

Retesting

Players may request another tier test 7 days after their previous test.

08

Fair Play

No cheating, hacking, exploiting, intentional interference, harassment, fabricated evidence, or false accusations.

09

Respect Your Tester

Do not argue with, insult, harass, or repeatedly dispute your Tier Tester's decisions during a test.

10

Final Notes

Tier testing is intended to evaluate skill fairly. Winning or losing is part of the process.
