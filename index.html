Community Rules

Keep the community competitive without turning it into a war crime tribunal.

1. Be Respectful

Be kind and respectful. No bullying, harassment, or hateful language.

2. Keep It Family Friendly

Keep conversations appropriate for all ages and avoid offensive content.

3. No Cheating

Hacks, cheats, unfair modifications, exploits, and encouraging cheats are prohibited.

4. No Spamming

Do not flood chats with repeated messages, emojis, stickers, GIFs, or excessive caps.

5. Voice Chat

Keep voice chat respectful and avoid shouting or disruptive noises.

6. Use the Right Channels

Use the correct channel for BedWars, general discussion, and off-topic content.

7. Advertising

Do not advertise other servers, websites, or social media without staff approval.

8. Fair Play

Play fairly, be a good teammate, and do not intentionally ruin other players' games.

9. Profiles

Keep usernames, profile pictures, and statuses appropriate for everyone.

10. Respect Privacy

Never share another person's private information.

11. Listen to Staff

Follow staff instructions and handle disagreements through the proper channels.

12. Reporting Issues

Use the correct channels and provide honest information when reporting problems.

13. Consequences

Punishments may include reminders, warnings, timeouts, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans.
