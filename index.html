Community Rules

Keep the community competitive, welcoming, and fun.

01. Be Respectful

No bullying, harassment, hateful language, or behavior that makes others uncomfortable.

02. Family Friendly

Keep content appropriate for all ages and respect different opinions.

03. No Cheating

No hacks, cheats, unfair modifications, exploits, or encouraging others to cheat.

04. No Spamming

Do not flood chats with repeated messages, emojis, stickers, GIFs, or excessive caps.

05. Voice Chat

Be respectful and avoid shouting, disruptive noises, or inappropriate microphone use.

06. Right Channels

Use the appropriate channel for BedWars, general discussion, and off-topic content.

07. Advertising

No advertising other Discord servers, websites, or social media without staff approval.

08. Fair Play

Play fairly, be a good teammate, and do not intentionally ruin another player's experience.

09. Profiles

Usernames, profile pictures, and statuses must remain appropriate for everyone.

10. Privacy

Never share another person's private information.

11. Listen to Staff

Follow staff instructions and handle disagreements respectfully through the proper channels.

12. Report Issues

Use the correct channels and provide honest information when reporting problems.

13. Consequences

Consequences can include reminders, warnings, timeouts, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans.

Join the Community

Meet other players, request tier tests, participate in events, and stay updated with everything happening in LBBW.
