Community Rules

  • Be Respectful: No bullying, harassment, or hateful language.
  • Keep It Family Friendly: No inappropriate or explicit content.
  • No Cheating: Hacks, cheats, exploits, and unfair modifications are prohibited.
  • No Spamming: Do not flood chats with repeated messages, emojis, or excessive caps.
  • Use the Right Channels: Keep conversations in their appropriate channels.
  • No Unauthorized Advertising: Do not advertise other communities without staff approval.
  • Fair Play: Play fairly and do not intentionally ruin other players' games.
  • Respect Privacy: Never share another person's private information.
  • Listen to Staff: Follow reasonable staff instructions and handle disagreements privately.
  • Have Fun: Protect your bed, help others, and enjoy the community.
