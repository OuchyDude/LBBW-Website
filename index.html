COMMUNITY RULES
Keep LBBW fun, friendly, and competitive.

1. Be Respectful

Treat everyone with kindness and respect. No bullying, harassment, or hateful language.

2. Keep It Family Friendly

Keep conversations appropriate for all ages and avoid offensive or explicit content.

3. No Cheating

Hacks, cheats, unfair modifications, exploits, and encouraging cheats are not allowed.

4. No Spamming

Do not flood chats with repeated messages, emojis, stickers, GIFs, or excessive caps.

5. Voice Chat

Keep voice chat respectful, appropriate, and free from disruptive noises.

6. Use the Right Channels

Use the appropriate channel for BedWars, general discussion, off-topic conversations, and other topics.

7. Advertising

Do not advertise other servers, websites, or social media without staff approval.

8. Fair Play

Play fairly and do not intentionally ruin another player's experience.

9. Profiles

Keep usernames, profile pictures, and statuses appropriate for everyone.

10. Respect Privacy

Never share another person's private information.

11. Listen to Staff

Follow staff instructions and handle disagreements respectfully through the proper channels.

12. Reporting Issues

Report problems through the correct channels and provide honest information.

13. Consequences

Punishments may include reminders, warnings, timeouts, kicks, temporary bans, or permanent bans depending on the situation.
