HOW RANKINGS WORK

Tier placement and leaderboard position are two different things.

Tiers vs. Wins

A player's tier represents their competitive skill and is determined through tier testing. The Top 10 leaderboard is separate and is based on verified wins. This means a player can have a high competitive tier without being in the Top 10, while another player can climb the leaderboard through wins.
